EV Lease Mileage Calculator

Product Definition & Iteration

The problem: two vehicle leases with different terms (36,000 miles over 3 years on one, 45,000 on the other) and no simple way to know whether driving habits were on pace. I defined the requirements around one plain-English verdict, then built a progress view against miles allowed by today, a per-day driving budget, and an end-of-lease pace projection. Later iterations added a second vehicle with its own terms and saved readings, driven by my own use.
